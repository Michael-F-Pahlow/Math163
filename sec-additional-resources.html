    Section 21 Additional Resources

    Subsection 21.1 Textbooks

    This course utilizes Openstax Calculus: Volume 2 (Chapters 5 and 6) and OPenstax Calculus: Volume (Chapters 2 to 5) both by Gilbert Strang and Edwin "Jed" Herman, et. al. Both text can be read online for free and downloaded as a PDF for free from openstax.org
    🔗
    Another excellent resource are Paul's Online Math Notes by Paul Davies at Lamar University. These notes cover math from algebra to differential equations and are great if you need a quick review of a topic. I have been using them since I was in college! Thankfully, Professor Davies has been updating and improving his notes since 2003.

    These notes include a pop-up GeoGebra-3D calculator. If you click on the calculator icon on the top of each page, then a GeoGebra-3D calculator will pop-up in the lower right of the screen. You can find more about the free GeoGebra calculators at https://www.geogebra.org/.
